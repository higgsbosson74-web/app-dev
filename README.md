# Arcane: League of Legends
 
**Arcane: League of Legends** is an animated steampunk action-adventure series created by *Christian Linke* and *Alex Yee*. Set in the universe of `League of Legends`, the story follows sisters **Vi** and **Jinx** as they find themselves on opposing sides of a brewing war between the wealthy, utopian city of **Piltover** and the oppressed undercity of ***Zaun***.

## Top 3 Reasons It Is My Favorite Series
 
1. **Groundbreaking Animation** – Animated by *Fortiche Production*, combining hand-painted 2D textures with 3D character models.
2. **Powerful Soundtrack** – Features an original score and hit tracks including *"Enemy"* by Imagine Dragons and JID.
3. **Rich Worldbuilding** – Explores the clash between Piltover's magical `Hextech` innovations and Zaun's dangerous `Shimmer` technology.
